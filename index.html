Privacy Policy

Luach · last updated 19 August 2026

Luach does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app sends nothing about you anywhere. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Luach keeps the following on your device only:

  • The city you choose, and your rite, language, theme and text-size settings.
  • Personal dates you add — birthdays, yahrzeits and other anniversaries — together with any names you attach to them.
  • People you save under “Loved ones”, including any Hebrew names you enter.
  • Which psalms you have marked as read, your favourites, and the collections you create.
  • Your blessing favourites and recent searches.
  • Your kashrut timer, if one is running.

This is stored in the app’s own storage on your device, and in a shared container that lets the app’s widgets show the same information. It is included in your device backups if you have those turned on, because it is part of the app. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, because the app has no server.

Location

If you tap “Use my current location”, the app asks iOS for your coordinates and uses them for two things: computing candle-lighting, havdalah and zmanim times for where you are, and pointing the compass toward Jerusalem.

The coordinate never leaves your device. It is not transmitted, not stored on any server, and not shared with anyone. The calculation runs on the phone. You can decline the permission and choose a city from the list instead; the app works exactly the same way.

The internet

Luach works entirely offline. The calendar engine, the prayer texts, the psalms and the blessing tables are all bundled inside the app. The app makes no network requests, so there is nothing for it to send and no third party to receive it.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

There are none. No analytics framework, no advertising identifier, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking of any kind — across apps, websites or otherwise.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it has stored. There is nothing held elsewhere to request or erase, because nothing is held elsewhere.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it.
